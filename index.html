Biography

I am an Assistant Professor at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), working at the Artificial General Intelligence Institute alongside Prof. Shaoqing Ren. My research focuses on Humanoid Robots and Embodied AI, spanning reinforcement learning, vision perception, LLM-based decision making, and control & planning.

Previously, I was Chief Researcher and Director of the Academic Committee at the Beijing Humanoid Robot Innovation Center (China National and Local Co-built Embodied AI Robotics Innovation Center), leading the TianGong team on frontier research.

Before that, I was a Research Scientist at Fourier Intelligence, a main contributor to the GR-1 humanoid robot and its reinforcement-learning-based locomotion control. I also worked as a Research Scientist at PNDbotics, a core contributor to the Adam humanoid toward anthropomorphic motion.

Earlier, I enjoyed a wonderful time working at DJI.

Students: I am looking for motivated Master/PhD students, undergraduate research interns, and RAs to join the group. If interested, please email me with your CV and a short research statement.
